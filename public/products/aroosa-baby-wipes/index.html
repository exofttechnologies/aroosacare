About Aroosa

Aroosa is a dedicated baby care brand founded with a singular purpose: to provide parents with pure, gentle, and trustworthy baby wipes for newborn and infant daily care.

Unlike multipurpose industrial wipes or third-party brands, Aroosa Baby Wipes are crafted specifically for sensitive skin with a 99.9% pure water formulation, skin-nourishing aloe vera, almond essence, and zero alcohol.
